# Neon Ice Slide 🧊✨

A neon sliding ice-cube puzzle game in a single HTML file. Swipe (or use arrow keys) to send the ice cube gliding — it keeps sliding until it hits a wall. Collect every star to clear the level, dodge the pits, and chain stars in one slide for DOUBLE / TRIPLE / MEGA combo bonuses!

## How to play

- **Swipe** or press **arrow keys / WASD** to slide the cube in a direction
- The cube slides until it hits a wall — plan your route!
- Collect **all stars** to clear the level
- **Pits** cost 1 life and reset the cube to its start tile (3 lives per level)
- Grab multiple stars in a single slide for **combo bonus points**
- Finish under **par** (or match it) to earn ⭐⭐⭐

## Features

- 30 handcrafted + verified-solvable levels with unique maze layouts
- Fireworks celebration on every level clear, grand finale on level 30 🏆
- Combo scoring, particles, sound effects (mutable)
- Persistent best score, unlocked levels, and per-level star ratings
- Mobile-friendly touch controls

## Run locally

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step needed.
